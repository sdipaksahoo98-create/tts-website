About Multi-AI Voice Studio Pro

Welcome to the ultimate Text-to-Speech (TTS) solution for Indian creators. Whether you are a YouTuber, a Reels creator, or making automated videos, our AI Voice Generator provides human-sounding voiceovers in a matter of seconds. We integrate powerful AI models like Sarvam AI to bring you the best regional language support.

Features Supported:

We support multiple highly requested Indian languages including Hindi, Odia, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Malayalam, Kannada, Gujarati, and Punjabi. You can customize the speech speed and choose between 30+ unique male and female voices.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How to convert text to speech in Hindi or Odia?

Simply type or paste your script into the Text Editor above. Select "Sarvam AI Cloud" as your engine, choose your desired language (like Hindi or Odia), pick an AI voice model, and click "Generate Voice".

2. Can I download the generated audio file?

Yes! Once the AI generates your voiceover using the Sarvam AI Cloud engine, a Download button will appear. You can download the audio as a high-quality .wav file directly to your device.

3. What is the character limit per request?

To ensure fast processing and prevent server overload, each request is limited to 500 characters. If your script is longer, you can easily break it down into multiple paragraphs, generate them one by one, and merge them in your video editor.

4. Are these AI voices free to use for YouTube?

Yes, the generated voices can be used in your digital content, YouTube videos, Instagram Reels, and educational materials. The output sounds highly natural and engaging for audiences.
